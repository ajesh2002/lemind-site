Simply Tokenized

Designing a tokenization platform that makes corporate finance feel simple

We partnered with Simply Tokenized to design the platform companies use to create, publish and manage tokenized offerings—and the investors who buy into them.

The product covers the whole journey: issuing a token, running the campaign, verifying investors and taking payment.

  • UX Strategy
  • UI Design
  • Web App
  • Design System
  • Payment Widget

Finance First, Blockchain Second

The people using the platform are finance teams, not crypto natives. Our challenge was to keep the power of tokenization while speaking the language of offerings, orders and accounts.

A calm, light interface with one clear action per screen sets the tone from the very first page.

A Guided First Offer

An empty dashboard is an invitation, not a dead end. One button starts the flow, and creating an offer comes down to a name, a symbol and a wallet—with the cost shown before you commit.

Every Offering at a Glance

Each offering is a card: name, network, visibility and contract address, with a colour edge and a status chip so a dozen tokens can be scanned in seconds.

One Token, the Whole Picture

The token page gathers contract terms, campaign dates and blockchain status beside a live table of orders, owners, whitelisted investors and payments. The next action sits on every row.

Confirm a payment or issue a token without leaving the list—the table is the workflow.

Investors, Verified

Regulated offerings depend on knowing who is investing. Accounts show KYC status, orders, portfolio and whitelisting together, and each investor has a full profile with wallets and change requests.

A System of Status

Tokens, orders, payments and accounts all move through states. One small set of colour-coded chips is reused everywhere, so a status reads the same on any screen.

A Payment Widget for Partners

Simply Tokenized also offers its checkout as a widget. Third parties embed it in their own sites to sell tokens and take payment, carrying their own logo with “Powered by Simply Tokenized” underneath.

  1. 01 Sign in With a password, a wallet or a social account.
  2. 02 Request whitelist Investors ask for access; an administrator reviews it.
  3. 03 Whitelisting confirmed The tenant approves and the investor is cleared to buy.
  4. 04 Buy tokens Choose an amount, pay by bank transfer or card, pick a wallet.
  5. 05 Payment summary What was paid, what arrived and where it went.
  6. 06 Investment complete A clear finish, with the new token balance.

Light or Dark, to Match the Host

Because the widget lives inside other companies’ products, it comes in two themes. The layout, steps and wording stay identical; only the surface changes.

Case study · Branding & Identity

Life Fitness

A gym identity built on a single bolt of energy—and a colour pairing that says wellness as loudly as it says strength.

Client
Life Fitness
Sector
Fitness & Wellness
Scope
Brand definition, Logo system, Colour
Applied to
Posters, Signage, Gym interiors
Brand The mark Lock-ups Colour Process In the space

The brand

Life Fitness combines the power of movement with the balance of wellbeing. The brief was to make it feel like a modern fitness and wellness destination—not just a gym.

Purpose

A stronger, healthier, more balanced life.

The fitness journey goes beyond physical transformation. Training is the means; a better everyday life is the point.

Positioning

A wellness destination, not just a gym.

Premium and calm where the category is loud and aggressive. Recovery and sustainable health sit beside strength.

Promise

Fitness Beyond Limits.

Unstoppable energy, forward motion, and the strength to push beyond every limit.

Values
  • Discipline
  • Focus
  • Energy
  • Results

Four words, set large on the gym walls—the brand speaks in short lines you can read mid-rep.

The mark

An L and an F, leaning forward and fused into one lightning bolt. Three strokes, three ideas—each part of the symbol carries a piece of the promise.

  1. 1

    Unstoppable energy

    The L strikes down the diagonal like a bolt—the spark that gets you through the door.

  2. 2

    Forward motion

    The top bar of the F leans into its italic angle, always moving left-to-right, never standing still.

  3. 3

    Strength

    The lower bar anchors the form and tapers to a point: stable at the base, sharp at the edge.

+ =

Lock-ups

One symbol, one wordmark, six ways to hold them together—so the logo stays itself on a shopfront, a phone screen or a dumbbell cap.

Give it room

Clear space on every side equals the height of the top bar of the F. Nothing enters it.

Keep the slant

Every edge shares one italic angle. Never straighten, stretch or rotate the mark.

Yellow is the bolt’s

On green or black the symbol is always yellow and the wordmark white—never the other way round.

Colour

Most gyms wear red and black. Life Fitness pairs a vibrant yellow with a deep green—distinctive and premium, and a signal that this place is about wellness as much as effort.

Green and black hold the space, white carries the words, yellow is the spark. Yellow stays under about a tenth of any layout—it works because it is rare.

Contrast by lightness, not by clash

The yellow is fully saturated and very light; the green is muted and dark. The pair gets its punch from the gap in lightness and saturation rather than from opposing hues, so it feels energetic without feeling aggressive.

What each colour says

Yellow is the lightest pure hue the eye sees, read as energy, optimism and forward momentum. Deep green is the colour of recovery and balance, and it recedes—calming the room so the bolt can lead.

vs

Against the category

Red and black signals intensity, and nearly every fitness centre uses it. Yellow on deep green is instantly recognisable on a street of gyms—and tells you this one cares about how you feel tomorrow.

Legibility, checked

Contrast ratios by the WCAG formula. All four pairings clear the 4.5 : 1 bar for body text.

Process

Four moves, from a crowded category to a system that works at every size.

01

Discover

We looked at how fitness centres present themselves and found the same answer almost everywhere: red, black and shouting. The gap was a calmer, more premium voice.

02

Define

We put the brand into words first—purpose, positioning, promise and four values—so every design decision had something to answer to.

03

Design

The initials became a bolt. A wide, forward-leaning wordmark was drawn to share its slant, and yellow and deep green were tuned as a pair.

04

Extend

Lock-ups, usage rules and two interior themes carry the identity from a poster to the gym floor—the bolt even becomes the lighting.

In the space

The palette becomes architecture: green columns and walls, yellow light lines that run the ceiling and floor, and the bolt as a beacon at the end of the hall.

Two themes, one palette

Dark: black ceilings and graphite floors let the yellow lines glow. The bolt stays yellow.

Light: white ceilings and daylight make the green feel fresh. The bolt turns to brushed silver on the feature wall.

Photography & campaign

Photographs are stripped to black and white, then washed in Deep Green, so any image belongs to the brand before the logo arrives. The bolt sits over the subject at poster scale, and the promise is always set in yellow.

  • Monochrome photography, green wash
  • One yellow element per layout
  • Bolt outline as a supergraphic behind type
