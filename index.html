Fintech · Web App

Simply Tokenized

Revolutionizing corporate finance with tokenization.

Client
Simply Tokenized
Industry
Fintech & Blockchain
Services
UX Strategy, UI Design, Design System
Platform
Web App
Year
2024

Overview

Simply Tokenized helps companies issue and manage digital tokens for real-world assets. They came to us with powerful technology but an interface only blockchain experts could use. Our job: make tokenization feel as familiar as online banking.

The challenge

Issuing a token involved dozens of technical steps, jargon-heavy screens and no clear sense of progress. Investors and finance teams struggled to trust what they couldn’t understand.

Our solution

We mapped every role in the platform, simplified issuance into a guided step-by-step flow and built a clean design system around clear data, status cues and plain language.

0% fewer steps to launch an offering
0× faster onboarding for finance teams
0+ reusable components in the design system

Agritech · Web Design

Akay

Taste nature through technology.

Client
Akay
Industry
Agriculture & Food
Services
Brand Website, UI Design, Content Strategy
Platform
Website
Year
2024

Overview

Akay connects conscious consumers with small farming communities, bringing fresh, traceable produce from field to table. They needed a digital home that tells the story of their growers as beautifully as it sells their products.

The challenge

Akay’s story was powerful but hidden behind a generic template site. Visitors couldn’t see where their food came from, and the shopping experience felt disconnected from the brand’s purpose.

Our solution

We built an immersive, story-led website with farmer portraits, origin maps and seasonal collections—paired with a simple, mobile-first shopping flow that keeps the human story front and centre.

0× longer time on site
0% increase in online orders
0 farming communities featured
